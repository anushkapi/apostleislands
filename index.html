Basic Facts About the Apostle Islands

LocationSouthwestern Lake Superior, off the tip of the Bayfield Peninsula, Ashland and Bayfield Counties, Wisconsin
EstablishedSeptember 26, 1970 (Public Law 91-424)
SizeAbout 69,377 acres (28,075 hectares) of islands, water, and mainland shoreline
Islands protected21 of the archipelago's 22 islands (Madeline Island, the largest, is not part of the park)
Mainland shorelineAbout 12 miles along the northwestern edge of the Bayfield Peninsula
Oldest exposed rockSandstone of the Bayfield Group, deposited roughly 1 billion to 660 million years ago
Official park sitewww.nps.gov/apis

All geologic ages, rock unit names, and glacial history on this site follow the National Park Service's Geologic Resources Inventory report for the park; full citations are on the References page.
